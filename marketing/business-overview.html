The Business

What Thrive with Tianna is

A nutrition and lifestyle coaching practice focused on helping adults achieve sustainable weight loss through low glycemic load (low-GL) eating — with real accountability, measurable progress, and human support.

The Problem We Solve

Most people are stuck in cycles of restrictive diets, guilt, and quick fixes that don't last. They need structure, education, and accountability — not another fad.

  • Confusion about what to eat for weight loss
  • Blood sugar spikes and energy crashes
  • No ongoing support between diet attempts
  • Lack of evidence that their approach is working

Our Solution

Tianna — a qualified low-GL weight loss specialist — delivers personalised coaching, meal plans, and group programmes backed by a client dashboard that tracks meals, projected glycemic load, and progress.

  • Science-based low-GL nutrition coaching
  • 1:1, group, and 30-day workshop formats
  • WhatsApp communities for daily accountability
  • Digital dashboard to log meals and see results

Services

What clients can buy

Launch offerings designed to get to market quickly. Clients cook their own meals using Tianna's plans — coaching and guidance, not food delivery.

Private 1:1 coaching with a personalised action plan, progress reviews, and messaging support between sessions.

From £199 / programme

£45 / month

Menu Plans

Personalised 1-week or 2-week low-GL meal plans with portions, shopping lists, and food swap guidance.

From £49 / plan

Low-GL Guidance

Ongoing monthly coaching on low-glycemic eating with dashboard progress tracking and meal planning support.

From £149 / month

Exercise Plans

Optional movement plans bundled with coaching packages — complements nutrition, not the primary offer.

WhatsApp Community

Every paying client joins a private WhatsApp group for their cohort. Daily check-ins, encouragement, tips, and Q&A — moderated by Tianna. This is the primary community channel at launch.

Target Audience

Adults (18+) who want sustainable weight loss without fad diets. Open to anybody ready for structure, accountability, and low-GL guidance. Not targeting children or families-as-a-unit.

The Platform

Client dashboard & insights

Beyond coaching calls and WhatsApp, clients get a digital hub to log what they eat and see evidence-based progress — giving Tianna data to personalise guidance.

Dashboard capabilities

Meal logging

Log daily meals with portion amounts. See projected glycemic load for the day and week.

Progress tracking

Weight trends, adherence scores, energy levels, and streaks over 7 / 30 / 90 days.

Achievement scoreboard

Badges, milestones, and tiers that celebrate consistency — encouragement, not punishment.
