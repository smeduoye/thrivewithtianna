My Story

From struggle to purpose

Tianna, aka Wumi

Hi, I'm Wumi — a qualified nutrition and lifestyle coach specialising in low-glycemic load weight management. My journey into wellness wasn't linear. Like so many of my clients, I spent years caught in the cycle of restrictive diets, guilt, and frustration.

Everything changed when I discovered the science of glycemic load — understanding how food truly affects our hormones, energy, and weight. I trained as a nutrition and lifestyle coach to share what I'd learned: that sustainable transformation isn't about deprivation. It's about nourishment, balance, and building habits that honour your body.

Thrive with Tianna is the space I created for people who are done with quick fixes and ready for real, lasting change. Whether you're managing weight, balancing hormones, or simply wanting to feel more vibrant — I'm here to guide you every step of the way.

"I believe everyone deserves to feel confident, energised, and at peace with food — without sacrificing the joy of eating." — Wumi

My Philosophy

The Thrive Method

01

Food as Medicine

Every meal is an opportunity to nourish, heal, and energise. I teach you to choose foods that work with your body, not against it.

02

No Diet Culture

We reject guilt, restriction, and quick fixes. Transformation comes from self-compassion, education, and sustainable habit change.

03

Whole-Person Wellness

Nutrition is just one piece. Sleep, stress, movement, and mindset all play a role — and we address them together.

04

Evidence-Informed

My approach is grounded in nutritional science — particularly glycemic load research — not trends or fads.
